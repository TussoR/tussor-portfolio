— 03 / About

Who I Am

Game design graduate
OCA Osaka · Class of 2022

I'm TussoR — a 3D artist and game designer with a formal education from OCA Osaka Design & IT College, Game Design program (graduated 2022).

Most of my freelance work is in the Roblox ecosystem — UGC items, character assets, and group commissions — but I've also contributed to projects with independent game studios, building game-ready props, weapons, and environments.

Every model is built to be practical and usable — reasonable poly counts, working UVs, and an honest effort to keep things tidy under the hood. Always learning, always improving. Currently open for commissions, studio collaborations, and long-term partnerships worldwide.
