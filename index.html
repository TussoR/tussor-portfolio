— 03 / About

Who I Am

Game design graduate
OCA Osaka · Class of 2022

I'm TussoR — a 3D artist and game designer based in TR. I graduated from OCA Osaka Design & IT College, Game Design program, in 2022.

Most of my freelance work is in the Roblox ecosystem — UGC items, hair, hats, weapons, and group commissions. I've also worked with indie game studios on game-ready props and environments.

I try to keep poly counts reasonable, UVs clean, and topology usable. I'm open for commissions and studio work.
